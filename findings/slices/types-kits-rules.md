# Types, the Kit Loader, and Rule Assembly

The review read all 72 files: 56 in `types/`, 13 in `kits/`, and 3 in `rules/`. 44 of them have new findings. The main patterns are these:

- Dead fields and dead return values.
- Shapes written two to nine times instead of named once.
- More hand copies of zod schemas.
- Two models of one thing: verbosity, "is a glob," and filename matching.
- Wrong or garbled doc comments.

Paths are relative to `packages/cli/src` unless they start with `packages/`, `tests/`, or `kits/` (the manifests in `packages/cli/kits`).
