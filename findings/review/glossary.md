How to read it:

- External API and format names stay as their owners spell them:
    - ESLint "rule," "preset," and "overrides"
    - npm and Bun "workspace"
    - Git "fetch" and "staged"
    - eslint-plugin-boundaries "element"
    - `vale sync`
    - Eta
    - Apple "asset catalog"
    - SPDX "license exception"
- The twelve CLI commands stay: init, install, apply, check, set, add, remove, export, ignore, list, explain, doctor.
- Where a record says kit, read configuration (owner decision of October 5, 2026). Record IDs such as `areas/kits/085` keep their spelling.
- Counts were measured on October 7, 2026, on the working tree at commit `3e1445a2d`, with whole-word matches. "src" is `packages/cli/src`, "plugin" is `packages/eslint-plugin/src`, "docs" is `docs/src`, and "tests" is `tests/`. Paths without a folder are under `packages/cli/src/`.
